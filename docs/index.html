For the SDR team

Download a Sales Navigator list to Excel.

Install once in Chrome. Open your people list. Click Download Excel. No Cursor, no terminal, no login extra step — it uses the Sales Nav session you already have.

Download for Chrome

Chrome or Edge. One-time install. Takes about a minute.

  1. 1

    Download and unzip

    Click the blue button. Unzip the file. You should see a folder named salesnav-list-save.

  2. 2

    Open Chrome extensions

    In the address bar paste chrome://extensions and press Enter. Turn Developer mode on (top right).

  3. 3

    Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked. Choose the unzipped salesnav-list-save folder (the one that contains manifest.json).

  4. 4

    Download a list

    Open a Sales Navigator people list. A navy card appears at the bottom right. Click Download Excel. The file lands in Downloads.

What it will not do

It only works on saved people lists (/sales/lists/people/…), not on search results. Stay logged into Sales Navigator in Chrome.
